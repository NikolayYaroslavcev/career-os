# Research Brief: Job Sources for Russian-Speaking Software Engineers

> Generated 2026-07-21 · depth: deep · workspace: research/job-sources-russian-sw/

## Research Question

What are ALL technically viable sources of software engineering job vacancies relevant to Russian-speaking developers worldwide, and how should they be ranked for integration priority?

## Scope Boundaries

### In scope
- Every job board, API, RSS feed, career page, community, and platform that:
  - Hosts software/IT job vacancies
  - Is accessible to Russian-speaking developers (Russian language content, CIS companies, or international companies hiring Russian-speaking engineers)
- Technical assessment of each source: API availability, auth methods, structured data, rate limits, legal considerations
- Scoring and tier ranking (S/A/B) based on quality, volume, relevance, and integration complexity
- Countries: Belarus, Kazakhstan, Georgia, Armenia, Serbia, Cyprus, UAE, Poland, Germany, Portugal, Spain, Netherlands, Czech Republic, Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Israel, Worldwide Remote

### Out of scope
- Implementation of any provider integration
- UI/UX design for displaying vacancies
- Salary benchmarking or compensation analysis
- Legal advisory on work permits or visa sponsorship

## Assumptions
- The platform targets software engineers (frontend, backend, full-stack, mobile, DevOps, data, ML/AI)
- Both remote and on-site positions are relevant
- Both Russian-language and English-language platforms are in scope
- Technical feasibility is weighted heavily — sources with no API and no scraping path get lower priority
- The research date is 2026-07-21

## Depth Mode
Deep — 5-8 initial sub-agents, 2 follow-up rounds, 25+ sources target
